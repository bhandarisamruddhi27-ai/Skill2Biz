🛡️ Trust & Fraud Awareness

Skill2Biz does not declare a person or company fraudulent automatically. It helps users identify common warning signs.

⚠️ Guaranteed Income

Be careful with promises of guaranteed or instant income.

⚠️ Unrealistic Returns

Very high returns with little effort can be a warning sign.

⚠️ Upfront Payment

Understand why a payment is required before joining a scheme.

⚠️ Referral Pressure

Be cautious if earning depends mainly on recruiting others.

⚠️ Unclear Information

Check product, service, seller and business information carefully.

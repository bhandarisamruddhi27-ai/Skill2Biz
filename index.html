SAFETY AWARENESS

🛡️ Trust & Fraud Awareness

Smart business also means making safe and informed decisions.

⚠️

Warning Signs

Be careful with unrealistic offers, suspicious links and unusual payment requests.

🔐

Protect Information

Never share passwords, OTPs or sensitive information with unknown people.

✅

Verify Before Payment

Check important details before making transactions or accepting unknown offers.

🛡️ Trust Principle

Skill2Biz creates awareness about common warning signs. It does not label a person or company fraudulent without evidence.

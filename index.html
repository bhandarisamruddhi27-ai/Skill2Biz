🛡️ Business Trust Check

Before joining an online business opportunity, check for these warning signs:

⚠️ Warning Signs
  • Large upfront payment demanded
  • Guaranteed income promises
  • Unrealistic returns
  • Strong pressure to recruit others
  • No clear product or service
✅ Safe Practice

Always verify the business, understand the product/service, check the terms and avoid making payments without proper verification.
